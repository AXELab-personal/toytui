# Contributing

This is primarily a personal hobby project. It is maintained for my own use,
and I work on it when I have time and interest.

## Bug reports

If you found a reproducible bug, please open an issue and include:

- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- The command or steps to reproduce it
- Relevant version information, if applicable

## Ideas and suggestions

I may consider well-described ideas, especially if they fit the project’s
existing scope and design. There is no guarantee that a suggestion will be
implemented.

## Pull requests

Small, focused pull requests are welcome if they fix a clear bug or improve
the project without significantly changing its design. Please open an issue
first for larger changes.

I may decline changes that alter the project’s intended purpose, add substantial
complexity, or require ongoing maintenance that I do not wish to take on.

## Forks are encouraged

If you have programming ideas, want to experiment with a different direction,
or need changes that do not fit this project’s scope, please fork the project
and develop your version.

A fork does not need my approval. Feel free to adapt, extend, or reshape the
project for your own needs, subject to the terms of its license.


## What I do not provide

This project does not include formal support, guaranteed response times,
or a commitment to implement feature requests.
